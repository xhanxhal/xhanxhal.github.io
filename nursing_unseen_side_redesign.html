The Unseen
Side of Nursing.

Nursing is built around care. But behind that care are realities that are rarely discussed: intimate clinical procedures, workplace violence, sexual harassment, night-shift insecurity, burnout, bullying, physical exposure and the emotional weight of illness and death.

This is about the difficult side of the profession — not about judging nurses.
The purpose is to make the hidden occupational risks visible.

02 / The most private part of the job

Sometimes, the job requires access to a patient's most intimate body areas.

Clinical reality

Male patient-এর genital area দেখা বা position/hold করতে হতে পারে

কিছু medical procedure-এ—বিশেষ করে urinary catheterization-এর সময়— patient's genital area-এর প্রয়োজনীয় অংশ expose করে clinical procedure করতে হয়। Catheter safely insert করার জন্য nurse-কে প্রয়োজন অনুযায়ী সেই area position বা hold করতে হতে পারে।

অর্থাৎ একজন female nurse-এর কাজের অংশ হিসেবেও male patient's private area দেখা বা clinicalভাবে handle করা প্রয়োজন হতে পারে।

Important distinction

“Private area” মানেই “sexual situation” নয়.

এটি medical care-এর অংশ। কিন্তু intimate care-এর সঙ্গে যদি inappropriate comments, deliberate exposure, unwanted touching বা অন্য boundary violation যুক্ত হয়, তখন situationটি nurse-এর জন্য psychologically uncomfortable বা unsafe হতে পারে।

A nurse may have to handle a patient's most private clinical needs — while being expected to remain calm, professional and emotionally composed.
একটি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ সীমা: Intimate procedure নিজে sexual বা unethical নয়। গবেষণা ছাড়া এমন দাবি করা ঠিক হবে না যে private-area-related clinical care নিজে থেকেই mental-health damage করে। Psychological burden-এর বিষয়টি বেশি relevant হয় যখন intimate care-এর সঙ্গে harassment, boundary violation, workload বা lack of support যুক্ত থাকে।

04 / When professional boundaries are crossed

Clinical proximity can be misused.

Inappropriate comments

Sexual jokes, suggestive remarks বা nurse-এর appearance নিয়ে inappropriate comments.

Unwanted touching

Clinical contact আর consent ছাড়া personal contact এক জিনিস নয়।

Deliberate exposure

কেউ ইচ্ছাকৃতভাবে inappropriate exposure তৈরি করলে routine care uncomfortable হয়ে যেতে পারে।

Sexual harassment

Professional setting-এ repeated sexual attention বা sexually inappropriate behaviour.

Physical assault

Threat বা verbal abuse কখনো physical aggression-এও escalate করতে পারে।

Serious assault

Sexual assault বা অন্য গুরুতর violence কোনোভাবেই “job-এর normal part” নয়।

05 / The second risk group

Sometimes the pressure comes from the patient's relatives.

Patient-এর condition খারাপ হলে family members-এর fear, anger বা frustration nurse-এর ওপর directed হতে পারে। Bangladesh-এর broader healthcare-worker research-এ relatives, friends এবং visitors-কে important perpetrator groups হিসেবে পাওয়া গেছে।

08 / The physical cost

The job can be physically exhausting too.

Blood & body-fluid exposure

Blood, urine, wound discharge and other biological materials can be part of routine care.

Needle-stick injury

Accidental sharps injuries are an occupational hazard in clinical environments.

Patient lifting

Moving or repositioning patients can create musculoskeletal strain.

Long standing hours

Extended periods on the feet can contribute to physical fatigue.

Infection exposure

Healthcare workers may face occupational exposure to infectious disease.

Recovery time

Long shifts and night duty can reduce time available for rest.

09 / The emotional burden

One patient dies. The next patient still needs care.

Nursing can mean repeatedly witnessing pain, deterioration and death. A nurse may support a grieving family and then return to another patient's care without having much time to process what just happened.

“Professional composure does not mean the person has no emotions.”

Repeated emotional labour can contribute to exhaustion and compassion fatigue; individual experiences vary.

11 / The hardest truth

Who protects the person who protects everyone else?

A nurse may be responsible for protecting a patient's dignity while working in an environment that does not always protect the nurse's own dignity. That contradiction is one of the most difficult parts of the profession.

The dark side of nursing is not that nurses are “bad”.
It is what the job can demand from the people doing the caring.

Protecting patient dignity

Even during intimate procedures, the nurse must maintain privacy and professionalism.

Protecting personal boundaries

Clinical responsibility should never mean accepting harassment or violence.

Protecting mental health

Safety includes psychological support, not only physical security.

12 / Evidence timeline

What Bangladesh research has documented.

2021

Registered nurses: workplace violence study documented physical assault, emotional abuse, threats, verbal sexual harassment and sexual abuse.

2021

Same broad nurse cohort: research examined workplace bullying, violence and burnout.

2022–2023

Healthcare professionals: nationwide study examined workplace violence, perpetrators, shifts and consequences.

2025

Nursing workforce research: qualitative evidence highlighted workload, night duty, security, sexual-harassment concerns and workplace challenges.
